**Settings…** (`⌘,`) is the knobs. On the Mac app it is in the **Label Lab** menu; on Windows, Linux and in the browser it is under **Edit**. They stick on this machine. They do not live in the `.label` file.

The dialog groups them in three parts. Clicking the **DPI** readout in the status bar opens it on Resolution.

### Output

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Resolution** | PSD raster resolution and pixel-ruler scale (72–1200 DPI, default 600). PDFs keep vector geometry and embed uploaded art at its original resolution. |
| **Bleed** | Extra around the trim. Geometry for both plates. Ruler zero stays at trim. |

### Can preview

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Corner radius** | Die-cut corner radius in inches (house die: 1/16 inch), shown on the 3D can. |
| **Edge softness** | Foil-edge blur in inches, shown on the can preview. |

### Appearance

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme** | System, Light, or Dark chrome. System follows the operating system. Also in View → Appearance and the `⌘K` palette. |

Bleed and DPI change what gets built. Corner radius and edge softness are how the can preview presents the press. They don't redraw the 2D plates.
