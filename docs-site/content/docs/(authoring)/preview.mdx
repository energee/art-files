title: Preview

One preview, centre of the window. View → **Label** / **Mask** / **Can** (keys `1` `2` `3`), or click **View Mode** in the status bar to cycle.

## Label and mask

Both plates draw in place. Drag the divider and the **white-ink layer** slides over the art so you can check registration. View → Label jumps the divider to artwork. View → Mask jumps it to the plate.

Live preview uses a downscaled texture and mask so typing doesn't churn print-size images. Downloads always rebuild at full resolution.

While export files are being built, the preview blurs and displays a progress note. Ordinary preview edits do not show this export indicator.

## Zoom and pan

- Scroll or pinch over the 2D preview to zoom about the pointer (up to 2.5×)
- **Right-button drag** pans (grabbing cursor). The browser context menu is off over the pane so this matches the 3D views
- The **percentage** in the status bar is a readout *and* a control. Type a figure. `100` fits the view. No separate reset button

In 3D, 100% means "as framed" for that mode. 200% is twice as close. Typing 100 restores framing distance and leaves your orbit alone. Each 3D view frames so the label reads the same on-screen height as the 2D sheet (measured to the label's near face). Switch views and the subject changes, not the scale. The can running off the top and bottom of the frame is the point. You're looking at the label.

## Can

Same plates, wrapped around a can. **1 / 4** in the status bar is one can or a 2×2 four-pack. Orbit with the left button and pan with the right. With the can view focused, arrow keys pan and Shift plus arrows rotates.

Settings → corner radius and edge softness are how the die-cut and foil edge read on the can. Preview/press appearance. They don't redraw the 2D plates, aside from bleed and DPI.

## Rulers and guides

View → **Rulers** (`R`) and **Guides** (`G`). 2D only.

- Ruler zero is the **trimmed** art's top-left, so bleed reads negative
- Click the trim-size readout in the status bar to cycle inches / points / 600-DPI pixels
- Locked margin and centre guides come from the trim box. The margin control (ruler corner) sets the safe inset on all four sides
- Drag from a ruler to add a **manual** guide, or use **Add vertical guide** / **Add horizontal guide** in the ruler-corner settings. Arrow keys move a focused guide by 1 pt (Shift: 10 pt). Kill it with the × in the ruler, by dragging it back into a ruler, or with Delete while it's focused. Manual guides snap to layers as you place them
- **Guides** is one switch: trim line, margin and centre, and your manual guides, together

Session-only overlays. They never enter a `.label` or an export.
