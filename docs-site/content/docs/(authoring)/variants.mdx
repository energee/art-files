title: Variants
description: Collab and other named looks, and which version you're editing
---

A **variant** is a named, toggleable diff on a master. It can override properties on existing layers and **add** layers that only exist while it's on. They stack. Turn on more than one.

The shipped Lolev master includes **Collab**. Off, nothing collab draws. That's every regular release. On, the partner block shows up: usually a lead-in line, a left-column logo under the description, and a centre logo under the laurel. Drop SVG, PNG, or JPG into those image layers. SVG takes the slot's fill, layer, and stroke. Raster art prints as-is and gets a white-ink underlay so it doesn't sit on the metallic texture.

## Two controls, two jobs

| Control | Job |
| --- | --- |
| **Chips** in the Layers header | Enable or disable the variant **on this label** |
| **Version** in the master picker | Where Save Master / promotion **writes** (base master vs that variant) |

Turning Collab on so this beer prints as a collab is a chip. Changing the Collab *template* is a picker version plus Save Master.

A layer a variant adds wears the variant's name as a badge and dims while the variant is off.

## New variant

Edit → **New Variant**, or New Variant at the foot of the master picker. Empty variant on this master, enabled on this label, and you're switched onto it so you can name it and add layers.

Rename or remove a version from the picker once you're on it.
