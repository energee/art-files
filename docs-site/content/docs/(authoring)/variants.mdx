title: Variants
description: Collab and other named looks, and which version you're editing
---

A **variant** is a named, toggleable diff on a master. It can override properties on existing layers and **add** layers that only exist while it's on. The master picker shows one working version at a time.

The shipped Lolev master includes **Collab**. Off, nothing collab draws. That's every regular release. On, the partner block shows up: usually a lead-in line, a left-column logo under the description, and a centre logo under the laurel. Drop SVG, PNG, or JPG into those image layers. SVG takes the slot's fill, layer, and stroke. Raster art prints as-is and gets a white-ink underlay so it doesn't sit on the metallic texture.

## Choose a version

Choose **Collab** under the current master in the master picker to show that version on this label. Choose the base master to return to the base version. The same choice sets where reviewed promotions in **Save Master** are written.

A layer a variant adds wears the variant's name as a badge and dims while the variant is off.

## New variant

**New Variant** at the foot of the master picker, or New Variant in the `⌘K` palette. Empty variant on this master, enabled on this label, and you're switched onto it so you can name it and add layers.

Once you're on a variant, the buttons under the picker **Rename** or **Delete** it, and **Save to Variant…** reviews and saves its changes (the same review as Save Master, written into this variant).
